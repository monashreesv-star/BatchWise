# BatchWise ERP - Eight-Minute Video Demonstration Script

---

### Timing & Scene Breakdown

| Timestamp | Topic / Demonstration Goal | Visual Action on Screen | Spoken Script / Voiceover |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00–0:40** | **Introduce BatchWise & Architecture** | Open Web Dashboard (`http://localhost:8000`). Highlight KPI cards and top navbar role switcher. | *"Hello everyone. Welcome to the demonstration of BatchWise, an industrial batch manufacturing ERP engine designed for FMCG and food processing operations. In standard commercial software, systems simply decrement stock counters. BatchWise implements an immutable double-entry stock ledger, a full mathematical BOM variance engine, role-based governance across five personas, and 360-degree batch traceability. Let's walk through the end-to-end business journey."* |
| **0:40–1:30** | **Salesman Places Retailer A Order** | Switch role to `Salesman (Ravi)` via the navbar dropdown. Navigate to `/sales`. Click `Instant Book Retailer A Order`. | *"First, we step into the shoes of our field salesman, Ravi Shankar. Notice our role badge is now Salesman. Ravi visits Retailer A Supermarkets and books an order for 100 units of Classic Bread 400g at ₹40 per unit. When we submit the order, the system automatically computes taxable value of ₹4,000, CGST of ₹100, and SGST of ₹100, totaling ₹4,200. The order enters 'Pending Approval' status. Notice that if Ravi attempts to approve the order or generate an invoice, the system raises an access violation—strict RBAC prevents unauthorized dispatches."* |
| **1:30–2:20** | **Admin Approves & Generates GST Invoice** | Switch role to `Admin`. Click `Approve`, then click `Generate GST Invoice`. Click `PDF` button to open invoice. | *"Now, switching to our Administrator role. The admin reviews the pending sales order, confirms pricing, and clicks 'Approve'. Next, the admin clicks 'Generate GST Invoice'. Behind the scenes, our allocation engine triggers FIFO batch allocation, verifies inventory, writes an immutable negative stock transaction to the double-entry ledger, updates Retailer A's outstanding customer balance to ₹4,200, and generates an official GST tax invoice. Clicking the PDF button displays our generated PDF 1.4 document complete with HSN codes, CGST/SGST breakdowns, reverse charge declarations, and bank details."* |
| **2:20–3:00** | **Purchase of Flour & Inventory Update** | Switch to `Purchase Manager (Priya)`. Go to `/purchase`. Click `Receive 1,000 kg Flour (PO + GRN)`. | *"Next, let's observe procurement. Switching to Priya Sharma, our Purchase Manager. We navigate to the Procurement tab. To replenish raw materials, Priya issues a purchase order for 1,000 kg of Refined Wheat Flour at ₹30 per kg to Golden Grain Agro Mills Ltd. When the delivery arrives, Priya generates a Goods Receipt Note, GRN-0001, linking vehicle number KA-01-AB-1234. The system assigns a unique batch lot number, RM-FLOUR-2026-B01, and records an inward ledger entry of 1,000 kg at ₹30 valuation into active inventory."* |
| **3:00–4:20** | **Production Run, BOM & Variance Analysis** | Switch to `Production Manager (Kumar)`. Go to `/production`. Click `Execute Baking Run`. | *"Now to the heart of BatchWise: the production engine. Switching to Kumar, our Plant Production Supervisor. In the Production tab, we inspect the standard BOM for Classic Bread 400g: 0.25 kg flour, 0.03 kg sugar, 0.02 kg yeast, and 1 pouch. Kumar creates a planned work order for 1,000 loaves. During the shift, the team consumes 258 kg of flour from batch RM-FLOUR-2026-B01, logging 8 kg of excess dough scrap, and produces 995 packaged loaves. Watch the variance engine calculate the exact numbers: standard flour for 995 output is 248.75 kg; actual was 258 kg; this generates a Material Quantity Variance of -₹277.50 unfavorable. Including direct labor and overhead, our actual unit cost is dynamically computed at ₹16.49 per unit, and batch FG-BREAD-2026-B01 is placed into finished inventory."* |
| **4:20–5:00** | **Distributor B Shortage Detection** | Navigate to `/inventory`. In the Shortage Tool, select `Classic Bread 400g`, quantity `1500`, click `Check Shortage`. | *"Next, we demonstrate automated shortage detection. Distributor B requests a large wholesale order of 1,500 loaves. We run our shortage intelligence engine: the system inspects active finished goods batches, detects only 895 units available, and immediately triggers an 'INSUFFICIENT STOCK WARNING' for a 605-unit deficit. Crucially, the engine doesn't stop at finished goods: it traverses the active BOM and computes the exact raw materials required to produce the shortage—in this case, 152.76 kg of flour including standard scrap allowance. This allows the plant to schedule production before committing to delivery."* |
| **5:00–5:40** | **Payment & Outstanding Calculation** | Switch to `Auditor (Anita)`. Go to `/invoices`. Click `Record ₹2,500 Payment`. | *"Now to financial settlement. Switching to Anita Menon, our Auditor and Accounts Manager. In the Invoices tab, Retailer A remits a partial bank payment of ₹2,500 against their ₹4,200 invoice via NEFT UTR-HDFC-991823. Anita records the payment. The system immediately adjusts the invoice status to 'Partially Paid', updates the remaining balance due to exactly ₹1,700, and updates the customer aging ledger in the 0-to-30-day bucket."* |
| **5:40–6:40** | **Costing & Batch Traceability** | Navigate to `/traceability`. Search `FG-BREAD-2026-B01`. Then search `RM-FLOUR-2026-B01`. | *"Traceability is essential for regulatory audit and food safety. In our 360-degree Traceability Matrix, we first trace finished batch FG-BREAD-2026-B01 upstream. One click reveals its baking work order, true cost breakdown, all four component batches consumed, and traces the flour back to Golden Grain Mills' GRN. Next, we simulate a mock food recall: if a supplier notifies us of flour contamination in batch RM-FLOUR-2026-B01, one click downstream instantly identifies every baking work order, every finished batch, and the exact customer accounts—Retailer A Supermarkets—who received those dispatches."* |
| **6:40–7:40** | **Monthly Audit Reports & Exports** | Navigate to `/reports`. Review Stock Ledger, GSTR-1, Variance tabs. Click `Export Formatted Excel`. | *"In the Audit & Compliance portal, we review our monthly stock ledger showing opening, inward, outward, closing quantities, and total inventory valuation. We switch tabs to view the GSTR-1 outward tax return with CGST and SGST totals, the production scrap log, and customer aging buckets. By clicking 'Export Formatted Excel', BatchWise downloads a native SpreadsheetML document formatted with bold headers, corporate blue headers, and currency formatting ready for statutory filing."* |
| **7:40–8:00** | **Explain the Technical Differentiator** | Show architecture diagram or live interview code in `engine/bom_production.py`. | *"To conclude: what sets BatchWise apart is engineering depth. By decoupling core dough recipes from packaging in our multi-pack BOM architecture, we can instantly add an 800g family pack sharing existing recipe calculations without duplicating code. Combined with our ACID-backed double-entry stock ledger, 100% database-verified benchmark tests, and strict RBAC, BatchWise represents a production-grade industrial solution. Thank you."* |
