## 1. Quick Recording Checklist & Setup

- **Browser Window 2 or Responsive Device Mode (Mobile View):** Press `F12` -> toggle Device Toolbar (iPhone 12 / 390x844px) at `http://localhost:8000/sales` to demonstrate the Salesman's mobile experience.
- **Audio:** Clear microphone, speak at a steady, confident pace.

## 4. Tips for High Scoring in the Evaluation

1. **Mention NIT Tiruchirappalli and Murukku 200g specifically** in the first 20 seconds. This immediately shows the evaluator that your solution was tailored specifically for their benchmark.
2. **Show Mobile View for Salesman**: The problem statement specifically asks for Salesman on mobile phone. Use Chrome DevTools (`F12 -> Toggle Device`) to show the responsive mobile order entry.
3. **Show RBAC Block**: When logged in as Salesman, clicking Approve or navigating to invoice creation shows an access restriction. This demonstrates role-based security.
4. **Highlight the Variances**: Draw attention to the `-6.0%` yield variance and `+5.0%` oil consumption variance; this is the core of Section 4.3 which carries the highest weight.
5. **Show PDF and Excel Downloads**: Download the PDF invoice and the Excel report to prove external accounting software is not required for daily operations.
