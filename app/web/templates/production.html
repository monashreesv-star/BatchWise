Technical Variance Accounting Engine
Material Quantity Variance (MQV):
(Std Qty for Actual Output - Actual Qty) × Std Price
Isolates operational floor inefficiency and scrap.
True Production Unit Cost:
(Actual Materials + Labor + Overhead) ÷ Actual Units
Establishes accurate FIFO cost basis for balance sheet.
Yield Variance Formula:
[(Actual Units Produced - Target Units) ÷ Target Units] × 100%
